ColorChute privacy

Last updated November 14, 2026

ColorChute is a small shirt art editor. You can use it without an account. This page says what it keeps about you if you sign in.

Without signing in

Designs you make stay in your own web browser. Nothing you draw or upload is sent to ColorChute's server.

When you sign in with Google

ColorChute asks Google only for your basic profile. It keeps:

  • your Google account ID, email address, name and profile picture link,
  • whether your account is waiting for approval or approved, and when you last signed in,
  • the designs and color options you save, so you can open them from any device.

A cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It is used only for that, and signing out ends it. ColorChute does not use ads or tracking cookies.

Who can see it

Your saved designs are visible only to you. The site's admins can see the account list (names, emails and approval status) so they can approve sign-ups. ColorChute never sells or shares your information. It is stored with Cloudflare, which hosts the site.

Deleting your data

You can delete any saved design yourself. To remove your account and everything saved with it, ask the admin who approved you, and it will be deleted.
