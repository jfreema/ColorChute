<h1 align="center">ColorChute</h1>

<p align="center">A simple layer editor for getting team shirt art to the printer.</p>

ColorChute helps you get 2 and 3 color baseball team and fan shirt designs ready to print. You bring in your art, try
different team color schemes, see how each one looks on a shirt, and save a print-ready file that meets Epic Sports'
requirements.

## What you can do

- **Try color schemes fast.** Fill areas, or swap one color for another across the whole design.
- **Keep every colorway.** Save a design once and keep several color options of it side by side.
- **See it on a shirt.** Preview on a plain square, a simple t-shirt or a realistic t-shirt, in any shirt color.
- **Send it to the printer.** Export a PNG with or without a background, sized and checked against Epic Sports' specs.

It runs in the web browser, works offline once loaded, and saves your designs in the browser on your own computer.

Built with TypeScript, React and Vite, and hosted as a static site on Cloudflare Pages.
